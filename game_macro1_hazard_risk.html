⚠️ Hazard, Risk or Damage?

MACRO 1 – Identify the correct safety concept in each scenario

🎯 Objective

You will read 8 workplace scenarios. For each one, decide whether it describes a Hazard, a Risk, an Accident, an Occupational Disease, or a Near Miss.

1️⃣Read the scenario
2️⃣Choose the concept
3️⃣Read the feedback
  • Hazard: An intrinsic property that can cause harm (a substance, machine, condition)
  • Risk: The probability that a hazard actually causes damage, considering exposure and controls
  • Accident: A sudden, violent event during work causing injury or death
  • Occupational Disease: A gradual illness caused by prolonged workplace exposure
  • Near Miss: An event that could have caused harm but did not
